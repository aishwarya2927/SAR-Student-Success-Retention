Beyond the formal scholarship process, the Institute runs a Student Financial Assistance Desk (Room 106, open Monday-Friday 11 AM-3 PM) for students facing short-term cash-flow problems that don't yet warrant a full scholarship application — for example, a delayed parental remittance, a pending part-time job payout, or an unexpected family medical expense. The desk can issue a short-term fee payment extension of 15-30 days without committee review, approved directly by the accounts officer, which is the fastest form of relief available and is usually the right first step for a student whose fee delay has only just crossed 30 days. For more sustained financial difficulty, the desk also coordinates with two external education-loan partners offering low-interest student loans, and maintains a small emergency grant fund (typically INR 5,000-15,000) for one-time crises such as a family medical emergency, replenished each semester through alumni contributions. Students should bring any available supporting documents, but the desk is instructed to provide at least an initial short-term extension even before full documentation is ready, to avoid a student missing an exam-eligibility deadline purely over paperwork delays.
