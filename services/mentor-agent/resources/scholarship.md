Scholarship

Purpose

This resource covers the National Scholarship Portal's merit- and category-based scholarship schemes so mentors can direct academically strong or category-eligible students toward formal scholarship support, distinct from the short-term financial assistance pathway.

Available Support

Beyond general Post-Matric and Pre-Matric schemes, NSP hosts scheme categories directly relevant to engineering/technical students, including the Merit-cum-Means Scholarship for professional and technical courses (engineering, pharmacy, management, architecture), AICTE Pragati (for girl students, covering tuition, books, and computer costs), and AICTE Saksham (for students with disabilities, covering study material, equipment, and fees). All schemes are accessed through the same unified OTR registration and Fresh/Renewal application flow, with scholarship amounts disbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer once institute and state/district nodal officers verify the application.

Eligibility / Rules

Merit-cum-Means and most technical scholarships require the student to be enrolled in an AICTE/UGC-approved professional course and to meet the scheme's specific family-income ceiling (commonly in the ₹2,00,000–₹2,50,000 range, though this varies by scheme) alongside a minimum academic performance criterion where specified. A student may generally hold only one central-government scholarship per education level in a given year — dual benefits for the same level are typically disallowed, so students should check each scheme's "conditions for availing other scholarships" before applying to more than one. Renewal each year requires updated documents, including a fresh income certificate (not a reused prior-year certificate) and current academic records.

Recommended Faculty Actions


For a student who is academically strong but financially constrained, recommend the Merit-cum-Means Scholarship specifically rather than only the general Post-Matric scheme, since it is tailored to professional/technical courses.
For eligible women or students with disabilities, flag AICTE Pragati or AICTE Saksham respectively, since these are targeted schemes many students are unaware exist.
Warn students against applying for multiple overlapping central schemes in the same year, since dual benefits are usually disallowed and can cause processing delays or rejection.
Remind returning scholarship recipients that renewal is not automatic and requires a fresh income certificate and updated academic records each year.
