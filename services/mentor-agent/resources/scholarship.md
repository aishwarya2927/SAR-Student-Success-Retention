The Institute offers a Need-Based Fee Relief Scholarship and a Merit Scholarship, both administered through the Accounts and Scholarship Office in Room 105. The Need-Based Fee Relief Scholarship is intended for students experiencing delayed fee payments due to genuine financial hardship; students with fee delays beyond 30 days are automatically flagged for a financial counseling conversation and may apply for a partial fee deferment of up to 60 additional days, or a reduction of up to 25% of term fees pending document verification (income certificate, fee receipts, and a written request from a parent or guardian). The Merit Scholarship, separately, is awarded each semester to students in the top 5% of their branch by CGPA and does not require a financial application. Applications for fee relief are reviewed by a small committee within 10 working days, and approved cases are reflected in the student's account within one billing cycle. Students who are flagged as at-risk primarily due to a fee-delay factor should be directed first to this scholarship office rather than only to academic tutoring, since unresolved financial stress is frequently the underlying cause of attendance and performance decline.
