Qualcomm Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


CGPA 7.0+ generally expected, with zero active backlogs
Open to B.E/B.Tech/M.E/M.Tech in Computer Science, ECE, or EEE
2024–2026 batches typically eligible for Associate Engineer (Hardware/Software) roles


Selection Process


Online Technical Assessment (aptitude + core CS/Electronics fundamentals, coding/logic design questions)
Technical Interview Round 1 (DSA for software; digital design/computer architecture for hardware)
Technical Interview Round 2 (deeper technical depth — kernels, drivers, SoC design, or ML/NPU optimization depending on role)
HR/Managerial Round


Key Preparation Areas


C and C++ are dominant; Verilog/SystemVerilog essential for hardware roles
Digital design, computer architecture, and operating systems fundamentals
For ML roles: on-device AI / LLM optimization concepts (Snapdragon NPU-related)
Individual technical depth matters more than college pedigree at Qualcomm


Timeline


Hires via specific R&D-hub-based drives (Bengaluru, Chennai, Noida) rather than mass campus hiring; runs through the Aug–Feb season plus rolling off-campus openings; no service bond for freshers in India
