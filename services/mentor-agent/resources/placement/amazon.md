Amazon Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Minimum CGPA around 7.0–7.5 (70–75%) for SDE roles at most campuses (IIT cutoffs can run higher, NIT/other campuses sometimes slightly lower)
Open to CS/IT and other branches with strong programming ability
No active backlogs
Amazon is one of the more active mass campus recruiters among the "big tech" names, visiting a wide range of engineering colleges (not just IITs/NITs)


Selection Process


Online Assessment — 2 coding problems (~90 min total), medium–hard difficulty, plus work-style/behavioral questions
Technical Interview 1 — DSA-focused coding
Technical Interview 2 — DSA + Leadership Principles (behavioral)
Additional technical/bar-raiser rounds (4–5 total interview rounds for SDE-1)
Bar Raiser final review


Key Preparation Areas


Heavy DSA focus — comfort with medium-to-hard LeetCode-style problems is the single biggest filter
Amazon's 16 Leadership Principles — prepare STAR-format stories for behavioral questions
Object-oriented design basics for some roles
Mock interviews focused on explaining thought process out loud


Timeline


Runs within the standard campus placement season (roughly Aug–Dec, sometimes a separate Jan–Mar cycle for some colleges)
Applies via TPO-coordinated on-campus drive at most participating colleges; off-campus applications also open via amazon.jobs
