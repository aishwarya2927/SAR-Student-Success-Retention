Capgemini Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


60% minimum across 10th, 12th, and graduation (in line with other mass IT recruiters)
Open to B.E/B.Tech/M.E/M.Tech and related full-time degrees
No active backlogs


Selection Process


Online Assessment (aptitude, verbal, logical reasoning + coding/pseudocode)
Technical Interview
HR Interview


Key Preparation Areas


Quantitative aptitude and verbal reasoning
Basic programming logic and pseudocode-style questions
Core CS fundamentals (DBMS, OOPs)
Communication and cultural-fit readiness for HR round


Timeline


Regular mass campus drives through the Aug–Feb season, with additional off-campus hiring windows
