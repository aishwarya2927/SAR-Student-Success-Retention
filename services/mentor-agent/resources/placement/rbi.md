RBI (Grade B Officer) Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Bachelor's degree with a minimum aggregate (varies by stream — General/DEPR/DSIM have distinct academic requirements, with DEPR/DSIM requiring a postgraduate degree in Economics/Statistics respectively)
Age: 21–30 years as of the reference date (relaxations for reserved categories)
Indian citizen (or eligible Nepal/Bhutan/Tibetan-refugee categories as defined in the notification)


Selection Process


Phase I (Prelims) — Objective online test: Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, English Language, General Awareness (200 marks, 120 minutes, qualifying only — not counted in final merit)
Phase II (Mains) — Three papers: Economic & Social Issues (ESI), Finance & Management (F&M), and English (Descriptive); mix of objective and descriptive questions
Psychometric Test (non-scoring, precedes the interview)
Interview (75 marks)
Final Merit: combined Phase II + Interview marks (Phase I marks are not counted toward final selection)


Key Preparation Areas


Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, and English for Phase I
Economic & Social Issues and Finance & Management conceptual depth for Phase II — current affairs, RBI publications, and economic policy awareness are essential
Descriptive English (essay, precis, comprehension) practice
For DEPR/DSIM streams: Master's-level command of Economics or Statistics respectively


Timeline


RBI releases the Grade B notification annually (recent cycles: notification in late April, Phase I in June, Phase II in July, followed by interviews); a multi-month process from notification to final result
