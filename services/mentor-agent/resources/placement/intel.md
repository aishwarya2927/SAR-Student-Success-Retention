Intel Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Typically 6.5–7.0 CGPA (65–70%) minimum across academics, no active backlogs
Open to B.E/B.Tech/M.E/M.Tech in CS, ECE, EE (core hardware/VLSI roles favor Electronics/Electrical branches)
Final-year students and recent graduates eligible


Selection Process


Online Assessment (aptitude + core CS/Electronics technical MCQs + coding)
Technical Interview Round 1 (DSA/programming for software roles; digital logic, computer architecture, VLSI basics for hardware roles)
Technical Interview Round 2 (deeper domain-specific and project discussion)
HR/Managerial Interview


Key Preparation Areas


Computer architecture, digital logic design, and OS fundamentals
C/C++ programming; Python for tooling/automation roles
For hardware/VLSI tracks: Verilog/VHDL, semiconductor fundamentals
Strong grasp of processor/chip design concepts is valued even for freshers


Timeline


Runs campus drives at core engineering colleges (especially for VLSI/hardware roles) alongside off-campus software hiring, concentrated in the Aug–Feb season; India R&D hubs in Bengaluru and Hyderabad
