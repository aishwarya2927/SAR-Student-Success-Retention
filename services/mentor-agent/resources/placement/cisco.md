Cisco Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


CGPA 7.0+ (or 70%) generally expected; can rise to 8+ at top-tier colleges
Open to B.Tech/B.E./M.Tech/MCA in CS, IT, ECE, EE
No active backlogs


Selection Process


Online Test (aptitude — Logical Reasoning, Quantitative Ability; technical — Programming, DS, Networking, OS)
Technical Interview Round 1 (DSA, algorithms, networking concepts)
Technical Interview Round 2 (system design + networking protocols: TCP/IP, OSI model, routing)
HR Interview (managerial/behavioral fit)


Key Preparation Areas


Networking fundamentals (TCP/IP, OSI model, routing, LAN/WAN, network security) — Cisco's core differentiator
DSA (arrays, linked lists, trees, graphs, DP) in C++/Java/Python
DBMS and Operating Systems basics
For Network Consulting Engineer roles: microprocessors and hands-on networking troubleshooting scenarios


Timeline


Runs both on-campus drives and continuous off-campus recruitment across major Indian cities (Bengaluru, Chennai, Pune, Hyderabad, etc.), largely in the Aug–Feb window
