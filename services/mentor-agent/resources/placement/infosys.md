Infosys Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


65% / 6.5 CGPA minimum across 10th, 12th, and graduation
Open to B.E/B.Tech/M.E/M.Tech/MCA and related full-time degrees
No active backlogs


Selection Process


Online Test (InfyTQ / campus test — aptitude, verbal, programming MCQs)
Written Communication / Essay Round
Technical Interview
HR Interview


Key Preparation Areas


Quantitative and logical aptitude
Core CS fundamentals (OOPs, DBMS/SQL, OS basics)
InfyTQ certifications and HackWithInfy participation for higher-package tracks (Specialist Programmer, Power Programmer)
Clear, structured communication for the essay/HR rounds


Timeline


Large annual intake (~20,000 freshers) via InfyTQ platform and direct campus drives, concentrated in the Aug–Feb season
