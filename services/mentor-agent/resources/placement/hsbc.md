HSBC Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


60% aggregate / 6.0 CGPA typically expected across academics, with no active backlogs
Open to B.E/B.Tech/M.E/M.Tech/MCA/M.Sc (CS, IT, Electronics preferred for Trainee Software Engineer roles)
2024–2026 batches generally eligible for current fresher/early-career openings


Selection Process


Online Test (CoCubes/similar — Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, English, Technical MCQs, pseudo-code)
Coding Round (2 problems, easy–medium level)
Technical Interview (final year project deep-dive, SQL/DBMS, core CS fundamentals, "why HSBC" framing)
HR Interview (banking-domain awareness + behavioral fit)


Key Preparation Areas


Strong SQL & DBMS (joins, normalization, ACID properties) — heavily emphasized as a data-driven bank
Core programming (Java/Python) and basic cloud/distributed-systems awareness
Awareness of banking/fintech concepts and HSBC's global business lines
Communication and "ownership" framing for behavioral questions


Timeline


Runs both on-campus and off-campus drives (Trainee Software Engineer, Data Analyst roles) mainly out of Hyderabad and Pune hubs, through the Aug–Feb season and rolling off-campus windows
