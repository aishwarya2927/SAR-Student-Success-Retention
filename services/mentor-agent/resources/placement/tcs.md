TCS Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


60% minimum throughout 10th, 12th, UG/PG/Diploma
Open to B.E/B.Tech/M.E/M.Tech/M.Sc/MCA (full-time only; no part-time/correspondence)
No active backlogs; academic gap not exceeding 2 years; age 18–28

Selection Process


TCS NQT (National Qualifier Test) — Foundation Section (Numerical, Verbal, Reasoning Ability) + Advanced Section (Quant, Reasoning, Coding)
Technical Interview
Managerial Round (MR)
HR Round

Key Preparation Areas

Quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal ability
Basic-to-intermediate programming/coding (C, DSA fundamentals)
Communication and HR-round readiness
Tiered outcomes based on NQT score: Ninja (~₹3.6 LPA), Digital (~₹7 LPA), Prime (~₹9 LPA)


Timeline


Large-scale hiring (~40,000 freshers) with NQT drives running through the campus season (roughly Aug–Feb), plus off-campus NQT windows
