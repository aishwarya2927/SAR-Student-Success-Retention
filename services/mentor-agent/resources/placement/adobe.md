Adobe Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Minimum CGPA typically around 7.0+ / 70% for the Adobe Software Development Engineer track
Open to CS/IT and closely related branches
No active backlogs
Adobe (Noida/Bengaluru offices) is an active campus recruiter at a reasonably wide set of engineering colleges, including many non-IIT institutes


Selection Process


Online Assessment — coding problems (typically 2) plus CS fundamentals MCQs
Technical Interview 1 — DSA and coding
Technical Interview 2 — DSA + CS fundamentals (OS, DBMS, OOP) + project discussion
Hiring Manager round — deeper technical/project discussion
HR round


Key Preparation Areas


DSA (arrays, strings, trees, graphs, DP) at a medium difficulty level
Core CS fundamentals: OOP, OS, DBMS, computer networks
Project deep-dives — Adobe interviewers frequently probe resume projects in detail
Basic system design awareness for later rounds


Timeline


Runs within the standard India campus placement season (roughly Aug–Dec for Day 1/2 slots)
Adobe also runs a well-known summer internship program (Adobe University); strong interns often convert to pre-placement offers
