Morgan Stanley Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


UG (penultimate/final year), PG, or MBA students from relevant streams with strong academics (commonly benchmarked around 3.5/4.0 GPA equivalent)
Technology track: Computer Science/Engineering background with programming proficiency
Investment Banking/Markets/Wealth Management tracks: finance, economics, commerce or related backgrounds
In India, candidates can apply to up to 5 different roles via Morgan Stanley's campus/Global Programs portal


Selection Process


Online aptitude test (~45 min: numerical, data interpretation, logical reasoning, programming basics)
Programming/coding round (4-5 DSA problems, ~45-60 min) - technology track
Technical interview 1 (30-40 min - CS fundamentals: DSA, OOPs, DBMS, projects/resume)
Group activity (team-based task, e.g., story-building or a Lego exercise) - held when multiple candidates are shortlisted
Technical interview 2 (deeper DSA/system design questions)
Technical-cum-HR round (behavioral and career-goal questions)


Key Preparation Areas


Data structures, algorithms and system design (for Technology roles)
Core CS: OS, DBMS, networking, OOPs
Teamwork/group-exercise readiness - practice structured collaborative problem-solving
Resume depth - be ready to defend every line, including projects and internships


Timeline


Summer Analyst Program: 8-10 weeks, interns typically join in May
Full-Time Analyst Program (Technology): ~15-week structured global training starting around August, including a 4-week group project
Campus/internship applications generally open in the preceding autumn (roughly August-November)
