ISRO Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


B.E./B.Tech (or M.E./M.Tech) with at least 65% marks (or equivalent CGPA) in Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Computer Science, Architecture, or related engineering disciplines
Age limit: generally up to 28 years for Scientist/Engineer 'SC' posts (relaxations for reserved categories)
GATE score is NOT mandatory for ISRO's own Scientist/Engineer recruitment (unlike DRDO)


Selection Process


Online Application via the ISRO Centralised Recruitment Board (ICRB) portal
Screening based on academic performance / shortlisting criteria
Written Examination (objective-type, core engineering subjects for the relevant discipline)
Personal Interview
(Final merit for non-GATE candidates: written test + interview combined; postings across ISRO centers like VSSC, SAC, URSC)


Key Preparation Areas


Core engineering subjects for your branch (Mechanical, ECE, CS, etc.) at a GATE-adjacent difficulty level
Previous years' ISRO Scientist/Engineer question papers for pattern familiarity
General awareness of India's space missions and current developments in space technology
Analytical/numerical problem-solving under time pressure (exam is time-boxed, e.g., ~90 minutes)


Timeline


ISRO releases its Scientist/Engineer 'SC' recruitment notification periodically (not tied to a fixed campus calendar); final-year students can apply provided they meet qualification criteria by the time of final selection
