Razorpay Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Minimum CGPA around 7.0+ / 70% across 10th, 12th, and graduation
Degree in CS/IT/ECE or closely related field; B.Tech/M.Tech
No active backlogs; final-year students and recent graduates (within ~1 year) eligible
Campus presence is selective (Razorpay is a mid-size, engineering-culture-first fintech) — combination of campus recruitment, online application, and hiring challenges/hackathons


Selection Process


Online Assessment — coding problems (DSA-focused)
Technical Interview — DSA and coding, evaluated for problem-solving approach
Technical/System Design round — deeper technical discussion, system design for more senior-adjacent roles
Culture-fit round — communication, collaboration, ownership
(Where applicable) AI/workflow-integration round assessing use of AI tools in problem-solving


Key Preparation Areas


Strong DSA — Python, Java, C++, or Go are all accepted; depth in one language matters more than breadth
System design fundamentals, since Razorpay leans B2B/infrastructure-heavy (payment gateway, banking APIs)
Ownership-oriented behavioral prep — Razorpay's culture rounds explicitly probe this
Comfort discussing how you use AI tools in your workflow, given recent additions to their process


Timeline


Falls within the standard India campus season at colleges it visits (roughly Aug–Dec)
Also hires actively off-campus and through hackathons/hiring challenges (e.g. via Unstop) year-round
