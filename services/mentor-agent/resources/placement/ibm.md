IBM Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Minimum CGPA typically 6.0–6.5 / 60–65%, among the more accessible cutoffs on this list
Open to all engineering branches
Backlog policy is comparatively lenient (often 1–2 standing backlogs tolerated, subject to clearance before joining) — always confirm against the specific drive notice
IBM is a mass recruiter, visiting a very wide range of colleges across India, including most tier-2/3 institutes


Selection Process


Online Assessment — aptitude, verbal reasoning, and coding/logical ability sections
Technical Interview — programming fundamentals, DSA basics, CS fundamentals
HR / Managerial Interview — communication, role-fit, willingness to relocate


Key Preparation Areas


Aptitude and logical reasoning (a larger weight here than at product-first companies)
Programming fundamentals in at least one language (Java, Python, C++)
Basic DBMS/OS/networking concepts
Communication and HR-round readiness (role fit, career goals)


Timeline


Runs within the standard India campus placement season (roughly Aug–Dec), with IBM often among the earlier/Day-1-2 visitors at many colleges
Also runs dedicated hiring for roles like the IBM iX / consulting track with slightly different criteria
