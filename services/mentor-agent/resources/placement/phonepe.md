PhonePe Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Minimum CGPA around 7.0+ / 70%, with no active/present backlog
Primarily B.Tech students across branches, though CS/IT/ECE profiles are most common for engineering (Graduate Trainee) roles
Working knowledge of SQL, Excel, and a scripting language (commonly Python) is expected even at the eligibility stage
Selective on-campus presence — visits a defined set of colleges rather than a mass nationwide drive


Selection Process


Online Assessment — ~15 MCQs on aptitude, quantitative and logical reasoning (30 min), medium difficulty
Group Discussion — open-ended business/strategy topic, evaluated on clarity, structure, and listening
Technical Interview — coding round (3–4 problems, ~90 min, easy–medium difficulty, e.g. Codeforces 1300–1400 level)
HR / Culture-fit round


Key Preparation Areas


Quantitative aptitude and logical reasoning (the OA leans heavily on this, not pure coding)
Group discussion practice — structuring an opinion, engaging respectfully, reaching a conclusion
DSA fundamentals (arrays, linked lists, trees, sorting/searching) at easy–medium level
Basic SQL and data-handling skills, given PhonePe's data-heavy fintech operations


Timeline


Falls within the standard India campus placement season (commonly around Aug–Oct for many colleges)
Also hires via hackathons/hiring challenges (promoted via platforms like Unstop) outside the formal campus cycle
