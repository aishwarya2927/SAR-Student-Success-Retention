Wipro Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


60% minimum across 10th, 12th, and graduation
Open to B.E/B.Tech/M.E/M.Tech and related disciplines
No active backlogs


Selection Process


Online Assessment (aptitude + essay writing + basic coding) — via Wipro Elite National Talent Hunt (NTH)
Technical Interview
HR Interview
(WILP — Work Integrated Learning Program — is a parallel track combining a job with a part-time degree)


Key Preparation Areas


Quantitative aptitude and logical reasoning
Basic coding and programming fundamentals
Strong communication skills — Wipro places heavy emphasis on verbal/written communication
Awareness of Wipro's work culture for HR round


Timeline


Primarily hires through Wipro Elite NTH campus drives across the Aug–Feb season; WILP applications often run on a separate cycle
