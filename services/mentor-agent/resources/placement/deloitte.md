Deloitte Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Typically 60–65% or equivalent CGPA across 10th, 12th, and graduation (varies by role: Consulting/Analyst vs. Technology)
Open to a mix of engineering and commerce/finance graduates depending on the role (USI Technology roles skew CS/IT; Consulting/Analyst roles are open to broader disciplines)
No active backlogs


Selection Process


Online Assessment (aptitude, verbal, and/or technical/coding depending on role)
Group Discussion / Case Study (for Consulting/Analyst roles)
Technical Interview
HR / Partner Interview


Key Preparation Areas


Quantitative aptitude and logical reasoning
Case-study and business-problem-solving practice (for Consulting/Analyst tracks)
Core CS/technical fundamentals (for Technology/USI roles)
Strong communication and structured articulation of thoughts — heavily weighted in GD/interview rounds


Timeline


Recruits through campus drives (Analyst/Consultant and Technology roles) mainly in the Aug–Feb season, alongside off-campus and referral-based hiring
