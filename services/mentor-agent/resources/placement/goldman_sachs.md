Goldman Sachs Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Open to pre-final year, final-year and postgraduate engineering students across Indian engineering schools (Engineering Campus Hiring Program)
Less than 12 months of prior work experience for entry-level engineering roles
For Summer Analyst (internship): students graduating roughly 1-2 years out are eligible; applicants may apply to a maximum of 4 unique business-area/location combinations
Finance/business roles (Investment Banking, Global Investment Research, Asset Management) typically look for finance/economics/business academic backgrounds


Selection Process


Online registration with resume, CGPA and a short motivation write-up (~300 words)
Online assessment - aptitude/reasoning test with a reading comprehension and essay component (technical coding round added for off-campus/engineering tracks)
Group discussion (for larger applicant pools)
Technical interview round(s) - typically 3-4 rounds covering coding, CS fundamentals and analytical ability
HR/behavioral interview


Key Preparation Areas


Coding and data structures (C/C++/Java/Python) plus competitive programming practice
DBMS, OOPs and core CS fundamentals
Quantitative/analytical reasoning and reading comprehension
Behavioral prep using the STAR method; be ready to explain "why Goldman Sachs" and division-specific motivation


Timeline


Engineering Campus Hiring Program registrations generally open around March each year for that year's final-year batch (full-time joining around July)
Summer Analyst Program applications for India/APAC typically open around July for the following year's summer intake
Results communicated within a few weeks of the application deadline; joining dates vary by role and division
