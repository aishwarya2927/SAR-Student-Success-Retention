Salesforce Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Minimum CGPA typically around 7.0+ / 70%, consistent across 10th/12th/graduation
Open primarily to CS/IT/ECE branches; strong coding fundamentals expected
No active backlogs
Campus hiring in India (Hyderabad/Bengaluru offices) is selective and concentrated at a smaller set of invited colleges compared to mass recruiters


Selection Process


Online Assessment — coding problems + aptitude/MCQ section
Technical Interview 1 — DSA-focused coding round
Technical Interview 2 — DSA + CS fundamentals (OOP, DBMS) + project discussion
Managerial/Hiring Manager round
HR round


Key Preparation Areas


DSA at medium difficulty, plus solid OOP design fundamentals
SQL/DBMS basics, since many Salesforce roles touch data-heavy backend work
Familiarity with cloud/SaaS concepts is a plus, though not usually mandatory for freshers
Clear communication — Salesforce interviews weigh culture/communication fit noticeably


Timeline


Falls within the standard campus season where Salesforce visits (roughly Aug–Dec)
Internship-to-PPO conversion (Salesforce internship programs) is a common entry route in addition to direct campus hiring
