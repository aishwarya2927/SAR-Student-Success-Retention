Barclays Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Bachelor's degree (or equivalent) for Analyst-level roles; MBA/Master's/PhD for Associate-level roles - no fixed minimum GPA specified globally, but a strong, consistent academic record (2:1-equivalent) is expected
Open to students in their final/penultimate year depending on the specific program (Explorer graduate scheme, Expert technical scheme, internships)
Right to work in the country of the role; in India, applications are typically routed directly through the campus placement cell
One application per candidate per recruitment season


Selection Process


Online application with CV and short screening questions
Online assessments (situational judgment test, behavior and ability assessments - varies by region/role)
Case study/presentation round (common in campus drives)
Technical interview(s) - DSA, SQL, DBMS, OS/CN fundamentals, and resume-based discussion for tech roles
HR/behavioral round (not always separately conducted; may be folded into the technical rounds)


Key Preparation Areas


Data structures & algorithms, SQL, and OOPs/DBMS/CN fundamentals
Case-study and presentation skills for business/operations tracks
Behavioral competencies aligned with Barclays' values (be ready with structured examples)
Time management for the online assessment stage - practice under timed conditions


Timeline


Full-time Graduate Programme: 1-2 year structured rotational scheme (Explorer/Expert tracks), typically starting mid-year
Summer internship: ~9 weeks, offered in India along with UK/US/Singapore/South Africa
Campus drives at Indian institutes are typically completed in a single day (assessment + interviews); average time-to-hire across roles is roughly 2-3 weeks post-application
