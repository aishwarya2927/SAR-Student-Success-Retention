Apple Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


No widely published CGPA cutoff; competitive profiles typically sit at 7.5+ CGPA
Strong preference for CS/EE/ECE backgrounds, especially for hardware-adjacent, systems, and ML roles
Apple's India campus presence is limited and highly selective — mostly IITs and a handful of premier institutes; most colleges do not get an on-campus drive
No active backlogs expected


Selection Process


Resume shortlist (referral or direct application carries significant weight)
Online Assessment / coding round (where applicable)
Technical Interview(s) — DSA plus role-specific depth (systems, ML, iOS/macOS, hardware)
Team-specific technical interview(s) — project and specialization deep-dive
Final/HR round


Key Preparation Areas


Strong fundamentals in DSA plus deep knowledge in your specialization area (systems, ML, embedded, mobile)
Ability to speak in detail about relevant coursework/projects — Apple interviews often probe depth over breadth
Low tolerance for surface-level answers; be ready to justify design decisions
For hardware/embedded roles: strong grasp of computer architecture and OS fundamentals


Timeline


No standard TPO-driven campus season for most colleges; hiring is largely off-campus and rolling
Where campus hiring exists, it typically follows global fiscal-year recruiting cycles rather than the local Aug–Dec/Jan–Mar placement windows
Best approached as an off-campus/referral target
