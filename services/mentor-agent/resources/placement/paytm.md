Paytm Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Minimum CGPA typically around 6.5–7.0 / 65–70%, though exact bar varies by drive/college
Open mainly to CS/IT/ECE branches for SDE roles; other branches considered for non-tech (ops, business) tracks
No active backlogs
Campus presence is moderate — Paytm runs both on-campus drives at select colleges and off-campus/direct hiring given its scale as a large consumer fintech


Selection Process


Online Assessment — coding problems + aptitude/logical reasoning section
Technical Interview 1 — DSA and coding
Technical Interview 2 — CS fundamentals + project/resume discussion, sometimes light system design
HR round


Key Preparation Areas


DSA at easy–medium difficulty, similar depth to other consumer-fintech recruiters
Basic system design awareness (given Paytm's scale, "how would you design X" style questions appear)
SQL/DBMS fundamentals — relevant given the transaction-heavy product
Resume/project depth — be ready to explain design choices and trade-offs


Timeline


Falls within the standard India campus placement season (roughly Aug–Dec) where it visits campuses
Also has ongoing off-campus hiring throughout the year via its careers page, since headcount needs fluctuate with business cycles
