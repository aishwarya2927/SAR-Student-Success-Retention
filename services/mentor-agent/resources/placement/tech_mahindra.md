Tech Mahindra Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Minimum 60-70% or 6.0-7.0 CGPA (no rounding up) across 10th, 12th and graduation, depending on the specific drive
Open to B.E./B.Tech, MCA, M.Sc (CS/IT); branches typically include CSE, ISE, IT, ECE, EEE, E&TC, E&I
No active backlogs at the time of selection; maximum 1-year education gap
Age typically 18-28 (some drives specify 18-25); 6-month cooldown if previously appeared for a Tech Mahindra drive


Selection Process


Online assessment (~90-150 min): aptitude/reasoning/verbal + technical MCQs + coding, sequential and elimination-based
English essay/writing round (topic-based, ~200 words, 15 min) - some drives
"Super Coder" test (optional, for top academic performers - unlocks the higher package track)
Technical interview
HR interview


Key Preparation Areas


Quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal ability (heavy weightage; sectional cutoffs may apply)
Coding fundamentals in C/C++/Java/Python (at least one coding problem is standard)
DBMS, SQL basics, OOPs, computer fundamentals
Essay writing/communication skills for the writing round


Timeline


Both on-campus and off-campus (AMCAT-routed) drives run through the year; campus drives concentrated in the July-December window for the graduating batch
Two package tiers commonly offered: standard (~₹3.25-4.5 LPA) and Super Coder (~₹5.5 LPA)
