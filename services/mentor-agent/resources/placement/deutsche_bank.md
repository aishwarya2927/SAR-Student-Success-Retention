Deutsche Bank Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Bachelor's or Master's degree from a recognized university in Engineering/CS/IT (technology roles) or Finance/Management/Economics (business roles)
Not more than one year of permanent work experience for graduate-level roles
No backlogs/pending coursework at the time of joining
In India, applications must be routed directly through the campus placement process (not the general online portal)


Selection Process


Application via campus placement cell (India-specific route)
Eligibility screen, followed by Behaviour and Ability Assessments (India skips the Situational Judgment Test used in other regions)
Online coding round (2-3 coding questions, sometimes combined with 5-10 MCQs on DSA/programming/OS)
Technical interview round(s) - CS fundamentals, DSA, low-level/high-level design for experienced/senior technical roles
In-person assessment day / group discussion, followed by a final HR or VP-level interview (on-campus in India)


Key Preparation Areas


Coding and data structures & algorithms (moderate-to-hard difficulty in online rounds)
Core CS: OOPs, DBMS, OS fundamentals
System design basics (low-level and high-level design) for more technical/senior profiles
Resume-based discussion and company/domain awareness for HR rounds


Timeline


Campus Hire and Technology Apprentice Program drives typically run through the second half of the year, with roles starting around September for that batch
The overall process (application to outcome) usually completes within about 6 weeks
Selected candidates are informed of interview outcomes within about a week of the final round
