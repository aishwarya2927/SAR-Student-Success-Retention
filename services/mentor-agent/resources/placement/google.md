Google Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


No hard CGPA cutoff published; 6.0+/60% is the nominal floor but most selected candidates carry 7.5–8.5 CGPA
Open to all engineering branches, though CS/IT and strong-coder profiles from any branch are preferred
No active backlogs; final-year students and recent graduates (within ~2 years) are eligible
Important: Google does not run a mass "campus placement" drive at most tier-2/3 colleges (including most colleges outside IIT/IIIT/BITS). Hiring is largely off-campus via direct application on Google Careers, STEP internship, or referrals


Selection Process


Resume shortlist / online application (Google Careers portal)
Online Assessment — 1–2 coding problems (medium–hard) in 90 minutes, sometimes with CS MCQs
Technical Interview 1–2 — DSA-focused (arrays, trees, graphs, DP)
System Design / Behavioral round (for SWE new-grad roles)
Hiring Committee review (packet-based, not interviewer-decided)


Key Preparation Areas


Strong DSA fundamentals — 200+ problems, comfortable with medium-hard difficulty
Competitive programming exposure (Codeforces/CodeChef/ICPC) is a strong differentiator
At least one substantial, well-documented project or open-source contribution
Clear articulation of approach and trade-offs under interview pressure


Timeline


No fixed India-wide campus season — Google posts openings on its own schedule (Careers portal)
STEP internship applications typically open in the final quarter of the calendar year
New-grad SWE (L3) hiring is largely off-campus/rolling; treat Google as an off-campus target rather than a scheduled TPO drive
