NVIDIA Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


CGPA 7.5+ generally expected (varies by program/hub, e.g., the N.Ex.T off-campus drive lists 7.5+ CGPA)
Open to B.Tech/B.E./M.Tech/M.E./MCA in CS, IT, ECE, EE; strong preference for Computer Science and Electronics
Zero active backlogs


Selection Process


Written/Online Test (aptitude, logical MCQs + technical questions: C/C++, OS, DS)
Online Coding Round (2-3 DSA problems, efficiency/optimization focus)
Technical Interview(s) (DSA, OS, parallel computing/CUDA concepts, GPU/hardware architecture for relevant roles)
HR/Cultural-Fit Round


Key Preparation Areas


Strong C/C++ and computer architecture fundamentals — valued more than trendy frameworks
DSA at LeetCode medium-to-hard level, plus systems-thinking (memory, caches, parallelism)
Basic CUDA/parallel computing awareness is a bonus, not mandatory, for freshers
For hardware/ASIC roles: digital design, computer architecture, Verilog/SystemVerilog basics


Timeline


Hires via the N.Ex.T (NVIDIA Excellence Trainee) off-campus program (open to 2026 B.Tech/M.Tech graduates) plus limited on-campus drives at top IITs/NITs; India hubs in Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad
