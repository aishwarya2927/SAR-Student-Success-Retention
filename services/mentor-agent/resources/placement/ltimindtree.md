LTIMindtree Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Consistent academic record of minimum 60% in 10th, 12th/Diploma, UG and PG (aggregate of all semesters/subjects, appropriate CGPA-to-percentage conversion applies)
Open to B.E./B.Tech/M.E./M.Tech (all engineering branches)/MCA/M.Sc-5yr integrated CS & IT
Only full-time/regular education considered; academic gap not more than 2 years
No standing arrears at the time of process; Indian nationals only
Strictly unplaced students (or those with a lower existing offer, per drive terms) are typically eligible to register


Selection Process


Registration through college placement cell (deadline-driven, drive-specific)
Online assessment (aptitude, reasoning, verbal - considered one of the tougher service-based company tests)
Technical interview (DBMS, SQL, OS, OOPs, and role-specific concepts)
HR/behavioral interview


Key Preparation Areas


Quantitative aptitude and logical reasoning (high difficulty relative to peers)
SQL, DBMS, and core CS fundamentals
Communication and behavioral round preparation
Flexibility/relocation questions - be ready to confirm willingness to work from any LTIMindtree development center


Timeline


Campus drives (branded "T-School" hiring) typically run August-September for graduating batches, with registration windows of a couple of weeks
Role offered is usually Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET); joining dates communicated after selection, based on business demand
