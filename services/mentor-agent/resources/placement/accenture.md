Accenture Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


65% / 6.5 CGPA minimum across 10th, 12th, and graduation
Open to all engineering branches and, for some roles, non-engineering graduates
No active backlogs


Selection Process


Cognitive/Online Assessment (analytical ability, English, logical reasoning, basic coding)
Technical Interview
HR Interview


Key Preparation Areas


Logical reasoning and verbal ability
Basic programming concepts (arrays, strings, linked lists, stacks, queues)
Teamwork, adaptability, and business-communication framing for interviews
Awareness of Accenture's service lines (Strategy, Technology, Operations) helps in HR round


Timeline


Runs frequent, high-volume campus and off-campus drives nearly year-round, with peak activity in the Aug–Feb season
