Swiggy Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria

65% / 6.5 CGPA minimum across 10th, 12th, and graduation
Open to all engineering branches; strong preference for CS/IT for SDE roles
No active backlogs

Selection Process

Online Assessment (90–120 min, DSA + debugging)
Technical Interview Round 1 (DSA, CS fundamentals)
Technical Interview Round 2 (system/low-level design)
Managerial / Team-Fit Round
HR Round

Key Preparation Areas

DSA (arrays, trees, graphs, DP) across Java/C++/Python/Go
System design and logistics-oriented problem solving
OOPs, DBMS, Operating Systems, Computer Networks basics
Behavioral prep aligned with Swiggy's values

Timeline

Both on-campus and off-campus hiring; concentrated in the core Aug–Feb campus season
