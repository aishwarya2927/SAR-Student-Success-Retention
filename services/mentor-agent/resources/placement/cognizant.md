Cognizant Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Minimum 60% (no rounding off) in 10th, 12th/Diploma, UG and PG for GenC/GenC Pro tracks; 70% minimum for GenC Next (premium developer track)
Open to B.E./B.Tech/M.E./M.Tech/MCA/M.Sc (all engineering branches; leather/food/fashion technology excluded); 3-year UG degrees (BCA, BSc, BCom, BBA, etc.) eligible for select roles
No standing backlogs/arrears at the time of the process; academic gap should not exceed 1-2 years
Only full-time/regular courses considered; Indian nationals/OCIs residing in India


Selection Process


Registration via college placement cell / Superset portal
Communication assessment (English proficiency)
Online technical assessment (~90-120 min: aptitude, coding, SQL, cloud fundamentals, no negative marking)
Technical interview
HR interview


Key Preparation Areas


Quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning and verbal ability
Core coding (1-2 problems) plus SQL queries
CS fundamentals: OOPs, DBMS, basic cloud concepts
Resume readiness with AI-tool/project exposure highlighted (Cognizant's 2026 drives favor GenAI-aware candidates)


Timeline


Large-scale campus drives run through the academic year, with major waves around July-September for the outgoing batch
Cognizant is targeting ~24,000-25,000 freshers for its FY26-27 cycle; results/offer communication typically follows within 3-5 weeks of the assessment
