NIC (National Informatics Centre) Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


B.E./B.Tech, M.Sc., MCA, M.E./M.Tech, DOEACC B-Level, or AMIE in Computer Science, Electronics & Communication, IT, or related disciplines
A valid GATE score (typically from the current or preceding two years, e.g., GATE 2024/2025/2026) in CS, EC, or DA streams is mandatory for Scientist-B
Age limit: generally up to 30 years for UR category (relaxations apply for reserved categories)


Selection Process


GATE Score-Based Shortlisting (no separate written test for most direct-recruitment Scientist-B posts)
Personal Interaction / Interview (technical knowledge, problem-solving ability, role suitability)
Final Merit: combined weightage of GATE score (~60%) and interview (~40%)
Document Verification


Key Preparation Areas


A strong, competitive GATE score in your discipline is the single most important factor
Core CS/Electronics fundamentals aligned with the GATE syllabus
For interview prep: awareness of e-Governance, India's digital infrastructure initiatives, and NIC's role under MeitY
For Scientist-C/D (experienced) posts: relevant research/development experience is assessed directly rather than through a written exam


Timeline


NIC releases Scientist-B recruitment notifications annually, generally timed a few weeks after GATE results are declared; application windows are typically short (around 3–4 weeks)
