Meesho Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria

70% / 7.0 CGPA minimum across 10th, 12th, and graduation (some drives cite 7.5)
Open to B.E/B.Tech/M.E/M.Tech in CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, E&I
No participation in Meesho's on-campus/lateral drives in the prior 6 months

Selection Process

Resume Screening
Online Coding Test (HackerEarth/HackerRank, ~60–105 min, 2-3 coding problems)
Technical Interview(s) (DSA + system design/LLD)
Hiring Manager / HR Interview


Key Preparation Areas
DSA fundamentals (arrays, trees, graphs, DP) — LeetCode/CodeChef practice
System design basics (caching, scalability, DB design)
Projects related to e-commerce/recommendation systems are a plus
SDE Trainee track: 6-month traineeship often precedes a full-time offer

Timeline
Open Campus Recruitment typically runs Feb–Mar for the upcoming graduating batch; SDE Trainee program based in Bengaluru
