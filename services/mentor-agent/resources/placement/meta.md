Meta Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


No published fixed CGPA cutoff; strong DSA/problem-solving ability matters far more than marks
Effectively targets top-tier institutes (IITs, IIITs, BITS) plus a small global University program intake; most colleges do not get a Meta campus visit
Final-year students / recent graduates via University Engineering roles or off-campus application
No active backlogs expected


Selection Process


Resume/profile shortlist (referral or direct application strongly helps)
Online Coding Assessment — 1–2 DSA problems
Technical Interview 1–2 — DSA (data structures, algorithms, problem-solving)
System Design round (for more senior/experienced-adjacent roles)
Behavioral round assessing collaboration and "Meta values" fit


Key Preparation Areas


Deep DSA practice — Meta interviews are consistently rated among the most DSA-intensive
Practice explaining and optimizing solutions under time pressure (typically 35–45 min/problem)
Behavioral prep around teamwork, conflict handling, ambiguity
Building a portfolio/GitHub presence, since off-campus and referral routes are the realistic path for most non-IIT students


Timeline


No standard India-wide TPO campus season; hiring is rolling/off-campus for the vast majority of colleges
University-level hiring events (where they exist) tend to align with global recruiting cycles rather than local placement calendars
Treat Meta as an off-campus/referral target for planning purposes rather than a scheduled on-campus drive
