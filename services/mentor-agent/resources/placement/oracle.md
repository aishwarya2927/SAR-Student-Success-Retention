Oracle Placement Guide

Eligibility Criteria


Minimum CGPA typically 6.5–7.0 / 65–70%, somewhat more accommodating than Google/Microsoft-tier cutoffs
Open to CS/IT/ECE and other branches for various tracks (Applications Engineer, Product Development)
No active backlogs
Oracle is a broad campus recruiter, visiting a large number of engineering colleges across India (including tier-2 institutes)


Selection Process


Online Assessment — coding + aptitude/logical reasoning MCQs
Technical Interview 1 — DSA and coding fundamentals
Technical Interview 2 — CS fundamentals (DBMS, OS, OOP) + project/resume discussion
HR round


Key Preparation Areas


DSA at an easy–medium level (less consistently hard than product-first companies)
Strong SQL/DBMS knowledge — heavily tested given Oracle's core database business
OOP and basic OS concepts
Aptitude and logical reasoning practice, since the OA often carries a sizeable non-coding section


Timeline


Runs within the standard India campus placement season (roughly Aug–Dec)
Oracle typically conducts multiple hiring cycles per year (Applications track and Product Development track sometimes recruit separately)
