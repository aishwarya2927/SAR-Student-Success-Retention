Probation

Purpose

This resource explains SPIT's academic promotion and standing rules so mentors can correctly identify when a student's backlog or credit-completion status places them at risk of detention or delayed progression, rather than treating every backlog as equally serious.

Available Support

SPIT's UG Academic Regulations tie year-to-year promotion to minimum cumulative credit completion rather than a single CGPA cutoff: promotion from First Year to Second Year requires at least 50% of credits completed at the end of the first year; promotion from Second Year to Third Year requires at least 70% of credits in the second year and at least 70% of first-year credits; and promotion from Third Year to Fourth Year requires at least 70% of credits in the third year, with a broader credit-completion threshold carried from earlier years. Degree completion requires a minimum of 160 earned credits with a CGPA of not less than 4.00, and the maximum duration to fulfill degree requirements is four academic years from first registration (subject to any extension rules for backlog clearance). Students with unresolved dues or pending disciplinary action can have their grades withheld even if academic performance is otherwise sufficient.

Eligibility / Rules

A student who does not meet the relevant year's credit-completion threshold is not promoted to the next year and must clear the shortfall before progressing; this is functionally SPIT's probation-equivalent mechanism, since it is credit-based rather than a separate "probation" declaration. Grade improvement is available in the same academic year for a CC–DD grade in the regular ESE, subject to a one-grade penalty, and does not apply to backlog (failed) courses, which instead go through re-examination. Students with attendance below 50% in a course are additionally restricted to the Special Examination on their first attempt only.

Recommended Faculty Actions


For any student flagged with multiple backlogs, check their cumulative credit percentage against the relevant year's promotion threshold (50% end of Year 1; 70% of Year 2 credits and 70% of Year 1 credits for Year 2→3; 70% of Year 3 credits for Year 3→4) before advising on urgency, since falling below this threshold is what blocks promotion, not the backlog count alone.
For a student close to the 4-year degree completion ceiling with unresolved backlogs, flag this early to the department, since timeline extensions are not automatic.
If dues or disciplinary matters are pending, clarify to the student that grades can be withheld regardless of academic performance, which may be the actual blocker rather than coursework.
Recommend the student review both credit-completion percentage and CGPA (minimum 4.00 required for the 160-credit degree threshold) together, since either shortfall independently affects standing.
