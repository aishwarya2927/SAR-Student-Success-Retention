A student is placed on academic probation if they end a semester with three or more active backlogs, or a CGPA below 5.5, or both. Probation status is recorded by the Academic Office and reviewed at the start of the following semester. While on probation, a student is required to meet their assigned faculty mentor at least once every two weeks, must enroll in any remedial or bridge classes offered for their weakest subjects, and has their attendance and internal assessment performance reviewed monthly rather than only at semester end. Probation is lifted once a student clears at least half of their active backlogs and brings their CGPA back above 5.5 in a subsequent semester; it is escalated to a formal academic warning, with parental notification, if a student remains on probation for two consecutive semesters without measurable improvement. Probation itself is not a punitive record — it is designed as a structured support mechanism, and students placed on it retain full access to tutoring, counseling, and scholarship resources alongside the additional mentor check-ins.
