The Exam Support Desk, run jointly by the Academic Office and the Tutoring Center, offers targeted help in the two to three weeks immediately before internal assessments and end-semester exams: previous-year question paper walkthroughs, last-mile doubt-clearing sessions, and short revision-plan templates for students managing multiple weak subjects at once. Students with two or more backlogs, or those flagged by a mentor as high-risk with an upcoming exam, are eligible for a personalized revision plan built in a single 30-minute session with an academic coordinator, prioritizing the subjects and topics most likely to affect pass/fail outcomes rather than attempting to cover everything. The desk also coordinates exam accommodations — extra time or a separate room — for students with a documented medical condition or disability, which must be requested at least one week before the exam through the Dean of Academics' office. Unlike day-to-day tutoring, exam support is explicitly short-term and high-intensity, intended for the final stretch before an assessment rather than ongoing conceptual learning, and mentors are encouraged to recommend it specifically when a flagged student's exams are within the next three weeks.
