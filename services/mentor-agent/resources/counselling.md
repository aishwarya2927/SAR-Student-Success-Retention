The Student Counseling Cell provides free, confidential one-on-one counseling for academic stress, anxiety, low motivation, family pressure, and general adjustment difficulties, and is staffed by two qualified counselors available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, in the Student Wellness Room near the library. Students can self-refer by emailing counseling@college.edu or by walking in during open hours, and a faculty mentor or any staff member can also refer a student with the student's awareness and consent. Sessions are not recorded in academic files and are kept separate from academic records; only a non-identifying summary (such as "student attended counseling support") is shared with a mentor if the student explicitly agrees to it. For students flagged through the early-warning system with high stress indicators alongside academic risk factors, the counseling cell offers a priority short-notice slot within 48 hours rather than the standard one-week waiting period. Crisis or urgent same-day situations should be directed to the cell's emergency contact line, posted outside the Wellness Room and on the college website, which is staffed at all hours during the academic term.
