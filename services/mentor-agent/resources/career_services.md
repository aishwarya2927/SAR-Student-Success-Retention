Career Services

Purpose

This resource describes SPIT's Training & Placement Office (TPO) so mentors can direct at-risk-but-improving students toward a concrete, forward-looking career conversation rather than only a list of academic problems to fix.

Available Support

SPIT's Training & Placement Office acts as the bridge between industry and students, offering consultation on employment, career planning, and available opportunities, and is responsible for training students ahead of recruitment drives. Training is split into an external track (aptitude and soft-skills training run by outside trainers) and an internal track handled by the TPO and departmental placement teams; the cell also organizes seminars, webinars, mock interviews, and presentations to prepare students for placement season. Some departments (e.g., IT, Electronics) additionally run their own placement cells staffed by faculty and students who provide soft-skill training and internship support specific to that branch.

Eligibility / Rules

Eligibility for a given recruiter's process is set by that company's own criteria (often a CGPA cutoff and no active backlogs), which the TPO shares with students once companies confirm their eligibility requirements. Students register with the TPO in advance of a placement drive and submit required documents to be added to the eligible list; the TPO also operates a "dream/super-dream/elite" tiering system based on offered CTC that governs how many companies a student may sit for once placed. Career guidance and training sessions run by the TPO are open to all students and are not conditional on being flagged as at-risk.

Recommended Faculty Actions


For a student who is otherwise stabilizing academically, recommend a TPO consultation or the department-level placement cell as a motivational anchor tied to a concrete future goal.
Point students early in their program (not just final-year) toward TPO-run soft-skills and aptitude training, since this preparation begins well before the placement season itself.
Where a student's branch has its own placement cell (e.g., IT, Electronics), recommend that department-specific resource in addition to the central TPO, since eligibility lists and soft-skill training may be organized separately.
Note that placement eligibility criteria (CGPA, backlog status) are set by recruiters, not the TPO, so a student with active backlogs should be advised on both academic recovery and the resulting placement-eligibility gap.
