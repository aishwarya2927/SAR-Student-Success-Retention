The Career Services Cell supports students with resume reviews, mock interviews, internship sourcing, and skill-building guidance aligned to their stated interest area, and is open Monday-Friday, 10 AM-5 PM, in the Placement Block. Students can book a one-on-one career counseling slot through the placement portal, where a counselor maps their current academic standing, GPA trend, and stated interest (for example, web development, data science, embedded systems, or core electrical work) to a short list of relevant skill-building suggestions, certification courses, or open-source/project ideas appropriate to their year of study. For students who are otherwise on track academically but unsure of direction, the cell also runs a short aptitude-and-interest assessment each semester. Career services is intentionally kept separate from academic risk interventions — a student does not need to be flagged as at-risk to use it — but for students who are at-risk and improving, a clear career-readiness conversation is often a strong motivational anchor alongside academic support, since it gives a concrete forward-looking goal rather than only a list of problems to fix.
