Study Skills

Purpose

This resource describes IIT Bombay's institutional study-support and mentorship structures so mentors can recommend a broad, habit-focused intervention for students whose difficulty spans multiple subjects rather than one weak course.

Available Support

IIT Bombay's Student Mentor Program (SMP) runs the Department Academic Mentorship Program (DAMP), a student-run support system primarily for sophomores and above that hosts sessions and compiles resources to guide students through their academic years, alongside the Institute Student Mentorship Program (ISMP) for first-year students and the English Learning Program (ELP) for students who want to improve their English through activity-driven classes. As part of a broader wellness strategy, IIT Bombay has also introduced mandatory wellness workshops each semester (four per semester, roughly eight hours total), covering stress management and coping strategies, delivered by trained full-time staff alongside senior-student and alumni mentors.

Eligibility / Rules

DAMP and ISMP mentorship is available to all students in the relevant year band and does not require an at-risk flag to access; a student can also reach out directly to the Counseling, Training, and Outreach (CTO) cabinet of SMP for training-related or wellbeing-adjacent concerns. The mandatory wellness workshops are built into the curriculum for all students, so attendance is an institutional requirement rather than a discretionary opt-in, and missed sessions can typically be made up by attending an alternate scheduled session.

Recommended Faculty Actions


For a student whose GPA is declining gradually across several subjects rather than failing one specific course, recommend the DAMP mentorship channel as the more appropriate first step, since it is designed for exactly this broader-pattern case.
For international or language-transition students struggling across the board, check whether ELP-style language support is relevant before assuming the issue is purely academic content.
Since the mandatory wellness workshops are curriculum-embedded, verify the student's actual attendance at these sessions before assuming disengagement, since a missed mandatory session is itself a signal worth investigating.
Encourage the student to connect directly with their DAMP coordinator or SMP mentor rather than only pointing them to a resource page, since these programs work through named senior-student mentors.
