The Center for Learning Development runs a rotating series of short workshops on study techniques that are independent of any specific subject: active recall and spaced repetition, note-taking systems, managing procrastination, exam-time management, and effective group study. Workshops run for roughly 90 minutes, are held every alternate Saturday in the Seminar Hall, and require no registration — students can walk in. These sessions are aimed specifically at students whose difficulty isn't a knowledge gap in one subject but a broader pattern across multiple subjects, often visible as a declining GPA trend over several semesters rather than a single weak subject; in those cases, a study-skills workshop is usually a more useful first recommendation than subject-specific tutoring, since the underlying issue is how the student studies rather than what they don't know. The center also offers a self-paced online module covering the same material for students who can't attend in person, accessible through the LMS under "Learning Resources," and a short diagnostic quiz to help a student identify which specific study habit is most worth changing first.
