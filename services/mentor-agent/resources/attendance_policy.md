Attendance Policy

Purpose

This resource explains attendance requirements so that mentors can correctly interpret an attendance-related risk flag and know which follow-up actions are institutionally appropriate versus which require escalation to the Dean of Academics or HOD.

Available Support

Under SPIT's Academic Regulations, students must maintain 100% attendance separately in lectures, tutorials, and practical classes for each course, but a relaxation of up to 25% can be approved by the Head of Department for documented medical emergencies, family issues, or participation in extracurricular/co-curricular activities, workshops, competitions, or hackathons, provided prior written permission was taken for institute-level representation. Attendance is monitored on an ongoing basis so that shortfalls are visible well before the end-semester examination window, giving mentors a window to intervene early rather than after a student is already detained.

Eligibility / Rules

Regardless of relaxation, 75% attendance is mandatory in each subject to be eligible to sit for the End Semester Examination (ESE). Students who fall between 65% and 75% attendance are permitted to attempt the ESE but must report to the department one week after the exam and complete mandatory training. Students with attendance below 65% (and, separately, below 50% in the ongoing semester for a specific course) are detained from that subject's exam, though those detained with attendance below 50% may still be permitted to appear in the Special Examination on their first attempt. Any condonation or relaxation must be routed through the HOD/Dean of Academics with supporting documentation.

Recommended Faculty Actions


If a student's projected attendance is trending toward the 65–75% band, flag them for a mentor check-in well before the ESE registration deadline, since the 65–75% band still permits the exam but adds a mandatory post-exam training obligation.
If attendance has dropped below 65% (or below 50% in an active course), advise the student in writing to apply for HOD/Dean-level relaxation immediately, and ensure any medical or family-emergency documentation is filed promptly, since relaxation approval is discretionary and time-bound.
For students who received institute permission to represent SPIT at university/state/national events, verify that this permission was documented in writing in advance, as retroactive claims are not covered under the relaxation clause.
Encourage sustained monitoring rather than a single check, since the regulation ties eligibility to end-of-semester status, not a snapshot.
