Students are required to maintain a minimum of 75% attendance per subject per semester to be eligible to sit for end-semester examinations; students between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation through the Dean of Academics with a valid medical certificate or documented family emergency, while attendance below 65% results in detention from that subject's exam with no condonation possible. Attendance is tracked weekly through the LMS and is visible to both students and mentors in real time, so a sustained drop is flagged well before it becomes exam-critical. A sudden attendance drop of more than 15 percentage points within a single month, even if the student is still technically above the 75% threshold, is treated as an early-warning signal and triggers a lightweight check-in from the assigned mentor rather than a formal academic process — the intent is to understand the cause (health, transport, financial work, personal circumstances, or disengagement) before it becomes a bigger problem. Students recovering from a documented illness or family emergency can request a temporary attendance waiver for the affected weeks by submitting supporting documents to the class coordinator within two weeks of returning.
